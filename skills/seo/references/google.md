# What Google says, dated

Every line below was read on Google's own pages on 17 November 2026. Quotes are Google's words. Open the page again before quoting one to a client: Google edits these pages without notice. With no web access, quote it with the date above.

## Content

- **People first.** Google asks whether the page gives "original information, reporting, research, or analysis", and warns against "mainly summarizing what others have to say without adding much value" ([Creating helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)).
- **Commodity content.** Google's example of it is "7 Tips for First-Time Homebuyers"; what it asks for instead is "unique expert or experienced takes", written "based on what you know about the topic" ([AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)).
- **Who wrote it.** "Is it self-evident to your visitors who authored your content?" Google warns against "AI-generated headshots, made-up names, or false credentials". Of experience, expertise, authority and trust, "trust is most important" (Creating helpful content).
- **AI tools.** Allowed when the result meets the same standard: "It is critical to manually factcheck and review all AI-generated content for accuracy and trustworthiness before publishing" ([Using generative AI content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content)). A disclosure is useful "when it would be reasonably expected" (Creating helpful content).
- **Length.** "There's no magical word count target, minimum or maximum" ([SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide)). "There's no ideal page length" (AI optimization guide).
- **Keywords.** "Keyword stuffing is against Google's spam policies", and "Google Search doesn't use the keywords meta tag" (SEO Starter Guide).
- **Timing.** "Some changes might take effect in a few hours, others could take several months" (SEO Starter Guide).

## Spam policies that a content plan can break

Source: [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).

| Policy | Google's words | What it means for a plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scaled content abuse | "Using generative AI tools or other similar tools to generate many pages without adding value for users" | Four checked pages a month, not forty generated ones. One page per query, wordings included: Google's AI guide says a page per variation made "primarily to manipulate rankings" falls here. |
| Doorway abuse | "Having multiple domain names or pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page" | A city page exists only with content true of that city alone. |
| Link spam | "Excessive link exchanges ("Link to me and I'll link to you")"; paid links are fine "as long as they are qualified" with `rel="nofollow"` or `rel="sponsored"` | A link comes from someone who chose to give it. A paid one carries `rel="sponsored"`. |
| Site reputation abuse | Content "published on a host site mainly because of that host's already-established ranking signals" | A guest article goes where the trade reads, on a subject the author knows. |

## The page in search results

- **Title.** Descriptive, concise, unique per page. "There's no limit on how long a `<title>` element can be"; it is cut to fit the device ([Title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link)).
- **Meta description.** One per page, written for that page. "There's no limit on how long a meta description can be, but the snippet is truncated" ([Snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet)).
- **Article structured data.** "There are no required properties"; recommended: `headline`, `author`, `datePublished`, `dateModified`, `image` ([Article structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article)).
- **FAQ rich results are gone.** "The FAQ rich result feature is no longer shown in Google Search results" (Google's documentation updates, entry of 15 June 2026, read on the [FAQPage page](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/faqpage)). Write the questions as headings for the reader; `FAQPage` markup earns nothing in Google.
- **Two languages.** "Each language version must list itself as well as all other language versions." A translation is a duplicate only "if the main content of the page remains untranslated" ([Localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions)).

## Search Console

The Performance report has four metrics: clicks, impressions, CTR and average position, which is "the average position of the topmost result from your site" ([Performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553)). Google's AI features are counted in the same report, "within the 'Web' search type" ([AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)).
