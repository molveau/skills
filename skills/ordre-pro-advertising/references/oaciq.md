# Real estate broker and agency (OACIQ): website advertising rules

Regulator: Organisme d'autoréglementation du courtage immobilier du Québec (OACIQ). Rules read in November 2026. This is not legal advice.

Who is covered: the broker, the agency, and also "quiconque fait la promotion de services de courtage immobilier" (Loi a. 22 para. 2; Règlement a. 112 and 113). A web studio that publishes for the broker is not covered directly, but the content is.

Date reserve: the OACIQ advertising guide dates from June 2022. The regulation was rewritten by D. 173-2023 (current version). The articles cited in the guide ("article 22 de la LCI", "article 111 du Règlement") match the 2025 version, but the OACIQ may have published a newer guide. Check with the OACIQ.

## Rules

| Topic | Rule | Article | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broker name | State the name used, as registered on the licence. | Règl. a. 114, para. 1 | OACIQ regulation PDF |
| Broker licence | State the licence held with the mention "courtier immobilier", "courtier immobilier résidentiel" or "courtier immobilier commercial". | Règl. a. 114, para. 2 and a. 115 | same |
| Agency name | State the agency name (as on the licence), followed by its licence: "agence immobilière", "agence immobilière résidentielle" or "agence immobilière commerciale". | Règl. a. 114, para. 3; a. 116; a. 117 | same |
| Agency's borrowed name | The full name on the licence must always appear in full, with no short version. | Guide, section 2.1.3 (p. 10) | OACIQ guide |
| Broker practising through a company | The site is in the company's name; add its name and "société par actions d'un courtier immobilier" (or the legal abbreviation). | Règl. a. 115.1 | regulation PDF |
| Reserved title | Only a licence holder may use "courtier immobilier" or "agence immobilière". | Loi a. 2.1 | statute PDF |
| "Autorisé à exercer au Québec" | Optional on a website. | Guide, 3.2.3 and 3.2.4 (p. 29-30) | OACIQ guide |
| Broker photo | No photo more than 5 years old. | Règl. a. 113, para. 5 | regulation PDF |
| Titles ("spécialiste") | Forbidden to suggest being a "spécialiste". "Spécialisé en", "spécialiste", "spécialisations" are not allowed. "Courtage immobilier agricole" or "courtage de copropriété" remain allowed. | Règl. a. 113, para. 2; Loi a. 48; Guide p. 33-34 | regulation PDF, guide |
| Affiliations, diplomas, titles | Allowed if true and current (for example F.R.I. paid up, professional order in good standing, recognized diploma). | Guide, 3.3.4 (p. 36-37) | OACIQ guide |
| Awards, clubs, honorary titles ("Club du million") | Allowed only if the criteria are available on request, the place is stated and the year is specified. | Guide, 3.3.4 d. (p. 37) | OACIQ guide |
| Testimonials and client reviews | No specific rule in the regulation or in the 2022 guide (no occurrence of "témoignage" found). The frame that applies: no false, misleading or incomplete advertising, none that omits an important fact; no statistic without a source. | Règl. a. 112, 113 para. 4; a. 110 | regulation PDF. Position on testimonials: to verify with the OACIQ |
| Prices and fees (remuneration) | The broker may offer reductions of remuneration, benefits or gifts to promote their services. The advertised sale price of a property must be the price in the brokerage contract. | Règl. a. 39; a. 112 para. 4 | regulation PDF |
| Comparisons | Comparative advertising is not forbidden, but requires source, period, territory, truthful and untruncated data. "Les meilleurs" without detail is judged unacceptable. Do not disparage another licence holder. | Guide 3.3.4 e. (p. 38-39); Règl. a. 92 | guide, regulation PDF |
| Superlatives ("la meilleure équipe en ville") | Judged confusing and incomplete in the guide (example of a faulty team advertisement). | Guide, 6.2 (p. 62-63) | OACIQ guide |
| Guarantees | The service provided must match the advertising statements. A mention such as "100 % des clients satisfaits" is ambiguous and rejected without criteria. | Règl. a. 110; Guide p. 37 | regulation PDF, guide |
| Teams | The agency name must be obvious, near the team name, and the team name must not dominate. On a team photo, each person is identified with their agency; non-licence holders with their function. | Guide, 6.2 (p. 62) | OACIQ guide |
| OACIQ logo | Only a licence holder may use it, in one of the 3 official versions; the agency logo must clearly prevail; not on forms not edited by the OACIQ. Registered trademark. | Guide, 2.3 (p. 10) | OACIQ guide. Graphic standards: to verify on the OACIQ site |
| Domain name and email | Must have a logical link with the name under which the broker practises. Forbidden to cause confusion with another name or a known body (for example "chambreimmobiliere.qc.ca"). | Guide, 3.3.3.1 (p. 36); Règl. a. 113, para. 3 | OACIQ guide |
| Advertising a property | Allowed only with a written brokerage contract. Remove the listing from every site as soon as the contract expires. | Règl. a. 111; Guide 4.2 and 4.2.1 | regulation PDF, guide |
| Property sheet and property page | Must state: name of the mandated broker or agency followed by the licence (conspicuous typography, at least as large as the rest), sale without legal warranty, seller's statements, the broker's interest, a notice that it is not an offer. | Règl. a. 118 | regulation PDF |
| Other brokers' properties | May be shown only if the web user can clearly tell "Mes propriétés" from the others. "Toutes les propriétés" without distinction causes confusion. Keep the seller's agency or broker name on each sheet. | Guide, 4.1 (p. 42-43) and 5.1 (p. 50) | OACIQ guide |
| Links to another site | No confusion about having left the site; the link is not a recommendation. A suggested mention is in the guide. | Guide, 7 (p. 75-76) | OACIQ guide |
| "VENDU" mention | On a sign as soon as all conditions of the offer to purchase are met. Removal at contract expiry or at the deed of sale, unless the seller agrees in writing. "VENDU en X jours": the calculation is framed by the guide. | Guide, 4.4.1 (p. 46-48) | OACIQ guide. Current version of the rules: to verify |
| Sold price | Until the deed is published in the land register, the price is confidential: forbidden to make it public or use it in advertising without the written consent of buyer and seller. The chambers' "sold" database is not for advertising. | Guide, 4.4.2 (p. 49-50) | OACIQ guide. The OACIQ has changed these rules since (the guide cites article 203562 of the OACIQ site): to verify |
| Statistics | Every statistic must state its source. | Règl. a. 113, para. 4 | regulation PDF |
| Suspension or revocation | Remove all advertising from the site (including the agency's list of brokers) and from social media. | Guide, 10 (p. 88) | OACIQ guide |
| Social media | Same rules as any advertising: the guide says expressly that social media are covered (section "Internet [incluant les réseaux sociaux]", table of mentions, page number to verify). An OACIQ article "Social media and real estate brokerage: a few ground rules" exists but could not be read (HTTP 403). | Règl. a. 114 to 117; Guide, table of mentions | OACIQ guide; OACIQ page not read |
| Mandatory information to clients | Since June 2022 the broker must tell clients about the existence and mission of the OACIQ (off the site, but worth placing on it). | to verify (exact article not found) | https://www.oaciq.com/en/broker/how-to-talk-to-your-clients-about-the-oaciq/ (search summary, page not read) |

Practical summary of mandatory mentions on a broker's site page (from the guide, 3.2.4):

1. Broker name as on the licence + "courtier immobilier" (or résidentiel / commercial).
2. Agency name as on the licence + "agence immobilière" (or résidentielle / commerciale).
3. For a broker practising through a company: company name + "société par actions d'un courtier immobilier".
4. For a property sheet: the content of a. 118.

The licence number is expressed through the licence-type mention (the "permis dont il est titulaire" of a. 114, para. 2). The text read does not require a numeric licence number on the site. Whether the OACIQ asks for one in practice: to verify.

## Contact form and Law 25 (broker)

For privacy mechanics use the `loi-25-website` skill. Points specific to a broker: the Règlement C-73.2, r. 1 imposes confidentiality on licence holders (a. 31), use of personal information only for the intended purposes (a. 32), and the duty to ensure their work tools preserve confidentiality (a. 33). A lead form on the site falls under these articles. Law 25 penalty figures were not re-verified here; check the Act or the CAI site before quoting them to a client.
