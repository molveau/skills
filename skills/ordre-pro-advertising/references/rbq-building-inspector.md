# Building inspector (Régie du bâtiment du Québec): website advertising rules

Regulator: Régie du bâtiment du Québec (RBQ). It is not a professional order (not under the Office des professions). Rules read in November 2026. This is not legal advice.

Article numbers are those of the Règlement sur l'encadrement des inspecteurs en bâtiments d'habitation pour les inspections en vue d'une transaction immobilière (REIBH), RLRQ c. B-1.1, r. 3.1, as published in the Gazette officielle on 28 February 2024. The consolidated LégisQuébec version (current to 30 November 2024 per a search result) should carry the same numbering. ## Regime and dates (what is mandatory in November 2026)

- The regulation came into force on 1 October 2024 (a. 46). Section IV of chapter III (continuing education, 20 h per 2-year period) comes into force on 1 January 2027 (a. 46 and 30).
- An RBQ certificate is mandatory for any individual doing an inspection covered by BNQ 3009-500 (a. 3), employees and self-employed alike.
- Transition: no certificate needed until 30 September 2027 (a. 43). In November 2026 the certificate is therefore not yet mandatory; the RBQ says "obligatoire à compter du 1er octobre 2027".
- To use the transitional route, file the application at least 60 days before 30 September 2027 (a. 44 and 45). The RBQ gives the transition period for applications as "du 1er octobre 2024 au 2 août 2027".
- Two categories: category 1 (small residential buildings of 1 to 6 units and private parts) and category 2 (any residential building) (a. 4).
- Qualification: AEC in building inspection of at least 600 h (a. 5). Transition: 30 h upgrade course plus insurance of experience (a. 44 and 45).
- Insurance: general civil liability and professional "errors and omissions" liability specific to inspector duties. Minimum per the RBQ: $1,000,000 (category 1) and $2,000,000 (category 2), for the duration of the certificate (a. 5 para. 3, and RBQ "financial guarantees" page). The amounts were not isolated in the text of a. 5: to verify.
- The certificate is valid 1 year, renewable (a. 14 and a. 9).
- A pre-purchase inspection remains optional for the buyer. The 2022 draft regulation that would have required it for buildings 25 years and older is not in the final regulation read. The RBQ only "recommends" an inspection.

### Associations (AIBQ, ANIEB)

- The AIBQ (Association des inspecteurs en bâtiments du Québec) exists and took part in the BNQ 3009-500 standard and in the debate on regulation. It is one of the recognized providers of the upgrade course: the regulation allows "une association d'inspecteurs en bâtiments ayant son siège au Québec" as provider (a. 44). A 2021 press release (AIBQ with InterNACHI-Québec) mentions about 800 members.
- No association is a legal condition. Membership does not replace the RBQ certificate. The regulation has no rule on displaying an association logo.
- Practical rule: an association logo on a site is a statement of fact. It must be true and current (general advertising rule). Do not present it as an "official title".

## Contact form and Law 25 (inspector)

For privacy mechanics use the `loi-25-website` skill. Points specific to an inspector:

- An inspection report contains the building address, the owner's and client's names, photos of the interior: all personal information.
- Do not give the report to a third party without the client's prior written consent, except: legal verification or inquiry, a body with jurisdictional functions, a court order, defence against a claim (Règl. a. 41).
- The 6-year retention of the file (a. 40) must show in the retention policy required by P-39.1 a. 3.2.
- If the site offers a booking or report-upload portal: hosting, transfer outside Québec (P-39.1 a. 17) and consent (a. 14).
- Photos of the property and its occupants on the site (portfolio) need the consent of the client and the building owner, or blurring.

## Uncertain

- LégisQuébec was not accessible: the text was read in the Gazette of 28 February 2024. Amendments since were not checked. An amendment after 30 November 2024 is possible.
- Insurance amounts ($1M / $2M): read on the English RBQ page, not isolated in a. 5 of the regulation.
- No explicit rule found on titles ("inspecteur certifié"), testimonials, comparisons, prices, social media. a. 38 is the only advertising rule in the regulation.
- How a. 38 applies before 1 October 2027 to an inspector without a certificate: the text does not say. Ask the RBQ.
- Pre-purchase inspection is not mandatory per the sources read, but the 2022 draft that required it for buildings 25 years and older was not found in the final regulation. Check whether a newer text is in preparation.
- The text of BNQ 3009-500 is paid: its content (for example on report mentions) was not read.
- ANIEB: not found. AIBQ: no current membership rule read.
- Loi sur la protection du consommateur (advertising to a consumer): articles not specified here. To verify before discussing with the client.
- RBQ delays as of 2026-11-12: the RBQ is processing priority applications received before 22 October 2026 and ordinary applications received before 29 September 2026 (RBQ page). An inspector waiting for the certificate cannot publish it yet.
