Advertising of medically required (insured) services: a participating physician may bill the patient only the fees set by regulation and posted. "Aucune autre somme d'argent que celle affichée [...] ne peut être réclamée ou reçue d'une personne assurée, directement ou indirectement, pour l'obtention d'un service médical dans un cabinet privé" (LAM a. 22.0.0.1 para. 2). A clinic site must therefore not offer a paid "priority" or "subscription" for insured services provided by a participating physician. Have a lawyer validate before proposing a membership programme.

## RAMQ participation: what is in force in November 2026

Definitions (LAM a. 1):

- Disengaged professional (désengagé): outside the plan, accepts the agreement rate, the RAMQ reimburses the patient.
- Non-participating professional (non participant): outside the plan, does not accept the rate; "tous les patients assument seuls le paiement des honoraires".

In force (text read on LégisQuébec):

- Loi 83 (RLRQ c E-20.002, L.Q. 2025, c. 10), in force since 24 April 2025: a physician who wants to become non-participating "doit y être autorisé par Santé Québec" (a. 2). The application contains name and member number, current status, place of practice, reasons.
- Santé Québec criteria (a. 3): number of non-participants in the health region, effect on access to insured services, capacity to call on the physician. Refusal is mandatory if the physician is the only one offering an insured service in their region.
- End of the authorization regime: the authorization "prend fin le 24 avril 2027" (a. 8) and the chapter I provisions cease to have effect on that date (a. 21). The physician returns to the previous status (agreement or disengaged).
- Five-year rule: to apply for authorization a physician must have been subject to an agreement for five years (LAM a. 27). This does not apply to physicians already subject to an agreement before 24 April 2025 (E-20.002 a. 20).
- Notice of non-participation given on or after 1 April 2025: the physician stays or becomes again participating or disengaged on 24 April 2025 (a. 19). A notice given before 1 April 2025 stays valid (a. 18).

What a non-participating (or disengaged) physician must say or do for patients:

- Before claiming fees: give "des informations suffisantes sur la nature et l'étendue des services inclus dans le prix" and the validity period of the price, with explanations on the account and payment terms. Separate the fee from the price of supplies, devices, drugs and products (M-9 r 17 a. 105 para. 1).
- Display in view of the public, in the waiting area, the price of services, supplies, ancillary fees and care billed (a. 105 para. 2).
- Hand over a detailed invoice (a. 104 para. 3). Under LAM a. 22.0.0.1: a detailed invoice showing each uninsured fee and service, with a mention of the right to claim reimbursement of an illegal payment (a. 22.0.1).
- A reasonable advance may cover fees and costs (a. 106 para. 2).
- A non-participating physician's patient receives no RAMQ reimbursement (LAM a. 1 e) definition).
- Fines under LAM a. 22.0.0.1: $2,500 to $25,000 for failing to display or invoice; $5,000 to $50,000 for claiming a sum not displayed.

## Aesthetic medicine: CMQ guidance

- The CMQ distinguishes aesthetic medicine, aesthetic surgery and reconstructive plastic surgery. "Médecine esthétique" is not a specialty: a physician may not call themselves a specialist in aesthetic medicine (guide 2023-11, p. 8; C-26 a. 58).
- The physician must practise within their competence. Outside dermatology, plastic surgery, ophthalmology and ENT, they must inform the Collège if they start an aesthetic medicine practice (aesthetics guide 2020, section 2).
- The 2020 guide suggests a detailed quote signed before the procedure: procedure, products, cost, recovery, duration of effect (appendices I and II).
- To avoid, per the 2020 guide (p. 29-30): posters with the commercial name of an injected product, links to manufacturers or lending institutions, commercial logos, contests and draws, sale of products by a third party associated with the physician.
- The guide cites, for discounts, the example "10 % sur les injections de toxine botulinique par unité" computed from the posted regular rate.
- Public reminders: CMQ and OIIQ release of 30 May 2024 (https://cms.cmq.org/files/documents/Avis-et-prises-de-position/comm-soins-medico-esthetiques-20240530.pdf); joint CMQ and Ordre des dentistes release of 28 November 2025 (https://www.odq.qc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/20251128-communique-soins-medico-esthetiques-vf.pdf). Content of both documents not read: verify before using them.

## Remote consultation, booking and payment

- A physician subject to an agreement or disengaged is entitled to remuneration for an insured service provided remotely only in the cases set by regulation (LAM a. 22.0.0.0.3).
- Télésanté Québec, sheet 09 (October 2024): remote consultation outside Québec must stay exceptional and the professional must check with their order (https://telesantequebec.ca/wp-content/uploads/fiches_informatives/09-teleconsultation_hors_quebec_102024.pdf). Text not read in full.
- Online appointments: secured (HTTPS), per the CMQ web page.
- Online payment and deposit: possible for a non-participating or disengaged physician ("avance raisonnable", a. 106 para. 2). For an insured service provided by a participating physician: no sum other than those provided for and posted (LAM a. 22.0.0.1 para. 2). A site must therefore clearly separate insured services, uninsured services and ancillary fees.
- A detailed invoice is mandatory as soon as a payment is requested (LAM a. 22.0.0.1 para. 3).

## Law 25 and Law 5 for a private clinic

For privacy mechanics use the `loi-25-website` skill. Points specific to a clinic:

- Health information is sensitive (express consent, P-39.1 a. 12). Articles: 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.5, 8, 8.1, 9.1, 10, 12, 14, 17, 90.1.
- Loi 5 (R-22.1): "cabinet privé de professionnel" and "centre médical spécialisé" appear in Schedule II, para. 1° and 2°, so a private clinic is a health-sector body. All information held is confidential (a. 5) and may be used or communicated only as the Act allows.
- Forms to avoid: detailed reason for consultation in a free field, test results, photos of lesions, health insurance number sent through an ordinary web form, unencrypted email. For booking ask the minimum: name, contact method, type of service.
- Cookies and pixels: third-party profiling or geolocation cookies off by default (CMQ; P-39.1 a. 8.1 and 9.1). No Meta or Google Ads pixel on booking pages.
- Hosting: if data leaves Québec, an assessment before communication (P-39.1 a. 17). The medical file is kept by the physician; do not store clinical information in the studio's site or CMS.
- Publish the title and contact details of the person in charge of personal information on the site (P-39.1 a. 3.1).
