# Psychologist or psychotherapist in private practice (Ordre des psychologues du Québec): website advertising rules

Regulator: Ordre des psychologues du Québec (OPQ), https://www.ordrepsy.qc.ca. Ethics contact: the Bureau du syndic of the OPQ. Links consulted on 2026-11-12. This is not legal advice.

The Code de déontologie des psychologues neither forbids nor expressly permits testimonials, Google reviews, displayed prices, before and after, or superlatives. It sets principles (no false representation, proof, professional secrecy). The precise rules come from the Code des professions (a. 60.1 to 60.3) and from professional secrecy. No recent OPQ guide on advertising or websites was found.

Abbreviation: r 212 = Code de déontologie des psychologues (RLRQ c C-26, r 212); r 222.1 = Règlement sur le permis de psychothérapeute.

## Rules

| Topic | Rule | Article | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Binding advertising message | The service must match what the advertising says, which binds the professional. | C-26, a. 60.1 | https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/lc/C-26 |
| False representation | Forbidden to make a false, misleading or incomplete representation, especially about competence or the effectiveness of services. | C-26, a. 60.2; r 212, a. 7 para. 2 | same and https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/rc/C-26,%20r.%20212 |
| Falsely attributed advantages | Forbidden to falsely attribute an advantage or performance characteristics to a service. | C-26, a. 60.3 | same |
| Result guarantees | No text says "garantie de résultat". But any promise of effectiveness must be justifiable and not misleading. | r 212, a. 77; C-26, a. 60.2 and 60.3 | same |
| Justifying claims | The psychologist must be able to justify the skills or qualities they claim in advertising (effectiveness, extent of services, level of competence). | r 212, a. 77 | same |
| Comparisons and superlatives | No article names them. "Meilleur psychologue de [ville]" falls under a. 77 (cannot be justified) and a. 60.2. Disparaging a colleague's methods falls under a. 60 (r 212). | r 212, a. 77 and 60 | same |
| Testimonials and client reviews | No article treats them. Real risk: the psychologist must not reveal that a person is or was a client (a. 15 para. 3). A testimonial must therefore come freely from the client, without the psychologist soliciting or republishing it without a framework. To verify with the OPQ. | r 212, a. 15 para. 3°; a. 44 | same |
| Google reviews | The psychologist cannot answer a negative review by confirming the person is a client. Say nothing about the file. | r 212, a. 15 para. 3° | same |
| Pressure to consult | Forbidden to urge someone pressingly and without justification to use the services. Targets "last chance" offers and pressing discounts. | r 212, a. 44 | same |
| Keeping advertising | Keep a copy of every advertisement for 3 years after the last broadcast. Hand over to the syndic on request. A site must therefore be archived at each content change. | r 212, a. 78 | same |
| Title "psychologue" | Reserved to members entered on the OPQ roll with a valid permit. | C-26, a. 36 e) | same |
| Title "psychothérapeute" | Reserved to physicians, psychologists, certain members of other orders, couple and family therapists, and holders of the psychotherapist permit. | C-26, a. 187.1 | same |
| Double title | A psychologist who writes "psychothérapeute" must precede it with their reserved title: "psychologue, psychothérapeute". The title is never used alone. | r 222.1, a. 2 | https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/rc/C-26,%20r.%20222.1 |
| Title "docteur" | A psychologist holding a recognized doctorate may write "Dr" only before their name with the reserved title after the name, or after their name with the discipline of the doctorate. | C-26, a. 58.1 | https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/lc/C-26 |
| Specialties | To the researcher's knowledge no specialist certificate exists for psychologists. A practice area (anxiety, children) is described as a service offered, not as "spécialiste". "Spécialiste" without a certificate is forbidden. | C-26, a. 58 | same |
| Mandatory mentions | No article requires the name or permit number on a site. Good practice: full name, reserved title, permit number, office address. No express obligation found. To verify with the OPQ. | to verify | |
| OPQ graphic symbol | If used: matches the original, without suggesting the advertisement comes from the Order. | r 212, a. 79 and 80 | https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/rc/C-26,%20r.%20212 |
| Displayed prices and fees | No obligation to display the rate on the site. If displayed, it must be accurate (a. 7, 60.2). Fees must be fair and reasonable; explanations on the statement and the payment terms must be given. | r 212, a. 52 and 53 | same |
| Fee agreement before starting | Consent must cover the agreement on the fee amount and payment terms. To build into the booking form. | r 212, a. 11 para. 1° | same |
| Missed-appointment fees | Allowed by written agreement, on conditions agreed in advance. The amount cannot exceed the fees lost. | r 212, a. 54 para. 3° | same |
| Advance and deposit | An advance may cover only the expenses necessary to carry out the services, by written agreement. A general "dépôt de réservation" is not provided for. To verify with the OPQ. | r 212, a. 54 para. 1° | same |
| Interest on accounts | Allowed only after written notice to the client. | r 212, a. 56 | same |
| False receipts | Forbidden to issue a receipt falsely stating that services have been or will be rendered. Important for automatic deposit invoicing. | r 212, a. 55 | same |
| Online payment | No specific rule. Same rules on written agreement and fee clarity. The payment must not reveal clinical content. | r 212, a. 53 to 56 | same |
| Online booking | No specific rule. The data collected is private life (a. 14: collect only what relates to the agreed services). | r 212, a. 14 | same |
| Social media | Same rules as the site. Avoid tying the psychologist title to an advertisement for a product or service unrelated to psychology. | r 212, a. 75 | same |
| Commercial products and services | Abstain from taking part, as a psychologist, in an advertisement recommending a product or service unrelated to psychology. Claims about psychology products sold must have scientific evidence. | r 212, a. 75 and 76 | same |
| Public statements | Avoid exaggeration and sensationalism; state the limits and contraindications of methods. Useful for blogs. | r 212, a. 58, 59 and 61 | same |

## Remote practice (télépratique)

Main source: OPQ, "Télépratique ou pratique à distance" and "Points de repère en télépsychologie", consulted 2026-11-12 (links above).

- Client outside Québec: check with the regulator of the client's location whether a permit is required, even without going there.
- A professional physically in Québec who serves clients outside Québec (example given: Ontario) must register with that jurisdiction's Order or obtain "une autorisation spéciale d'exercer".
- Inform the client that the OPQ can handle a complaint even if they were not in Québec.
- The OPQ insurance coverage "n'est valide que pour des réclamations sur le territoire canadien".
- Get free and informed consent before remote practice, verify the client's location and a callback number, provide a safety plan (contact person, nearest emergency room).
- The full guide "Guide de pratique concernant l'exercice de la télépsychologie" dates from 2013 (link found on a third-party site, https://www.eer.qc.ca/shared/1598971872300/guide-de-pratique_telepsychologie.pdf). The more recent OPQ page still refers to it. Current text of the guide not read.

Consequence for the site: an "online consultation" page must say in which provinces the psychologist can see clients, and the booking form must ask the province where the client will be at the time of the session.

## Law 25 and health data

For privacy mechanics use the `loi-25-website` skill. Points specific to a psychologist:

- Loi sur les renseignements de santé et de services sociaux (Loi 5, RLRQ c R-22.1, L.Q. 2023 c. 5): it applies to "organismes du secteur de la santé et des services sociaux" (a. 4). Schedule II, para. 1°, includes "une personne ou un groupement qui exploite un cabinet privé de professionnel" within the meaning of G-1.021. G-1.021 a. 481 defines that practice as a business where "médecins, dentistes ou autres professionnels" practise privately. A psychologist in private practice therefore seems covered.
- Schedule II carries the mention "N.I. 2024-12-01". It could not be confirmed on the page read which paragraphs are in force and since when. To verify with the Ministry of Health or a lawyer: before writing that Loi 5 applies to a specific practice.
- A health and social services information includes any information that identifies a person and concerns their mental health or services received (a. 2). Name and contact details are also covered when collected for registration or intake (a. 2 para. 2). A booking form is therefore in scope.
- What a contact or booking form should not ask: the detailed reason for consultation, diagnoses, medication, history in a free field (r 212 a. 14: do not collect what has no link with the agreed services); a short optional "reason" box is enough. Health insurance or social insurance numbers. Information on third parties (spouse, children) without need. A bundled consent: separate consent to the privacy policy, to email or text reminders, and to the newsletter (P-39.1 a. 14).
- Hosting and tools: use booking and payment tools that state where data is stored; if outside Québec, do the P-39.1 a. 17 assessment. No advertising tracking pixels (Meta, Google Ads) on booking pages. The CMQ recommends third-party profiling cookies off by default (source for physicians, valid by analogy).
- Avoid a confirmation message that carries the professional's name and the word "psychothérapie" in the subject visible on a locked screen. Good practice, not a written rule.
- P-39.1 articles read: 3.1 (person in charge, title and contact published on the site), 3.2, 3.3, 3.5, 8, 8.1, 9.1, 10, 12 (express consent for sensitive information used for another purpose), 14 (minor under 14: holder of parental authority), 17 (assessment before communication outside Québec), 90.1.

## Uncertain

- No current OPQ guide on advertising, websites, testimonials, Google reviews or displaying rates was found. The absence of a written prohibition is not permission. To verify by phone or email with the OPQ's Bureau du syndic.
- The OPQ's position on testimonials published by the psychologist is unknown. The conclusion drawn from a. 15 para. 3° is a prudent inference, not a written OPQ rule.
- The content of L.Q. 2026, c. 8 (which touches r 222.1) was not read.
- Exact entry into force of Loi 5 for private professional practices (Schedule II): To verify.
- Obligation to state the permit number on a site: none found. To verify.
- The 2013 telepsychology guide may have been replaced; the current text was not read.
- No verification on a "dépôt de réservation" for future sessions: a. 54 para. 1° speaks only of an advance for expenses.
