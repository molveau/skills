# Mortgage broker (Autorité des marchés financiers): website advertising rules

Regulator: Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF), not the OACIQ. Mortgage brokerage is a discipline under the Loi sur la distribution de produits et services financiers (LDPSF, RLRQ c D-9.2): "Constituent des disciplines : ... le courtage hypothécaire" (a. 13), with the mortgage broker defined in a. 11.1. The Loi sur le courtage immobilier (OACIQ) does not cover mortgage brokerage. Rules read in November 2026. This is not legal advice.

The law distinguishes the broker (an individual with a certificate) from the firm (registered with the AMF, a. 71). A solo broker is either attached to a firm or a "représentant autonome". The advertising must name the entity that practises.

Abbreviations: r 2 = Règlement sur le cabinet, le représentant autonome et la société autonome (D-9.2, r 2); r 10 = Règlement sur l'exercice des activités des représentants (D-9.2, r 10); LPC = Loi sur la protection du consommateur (P-40.1).

## Rules

| Topic | Rule | Article | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who may call themselves a mortgage broker | No one may act as a representative, or present themselves as one, without an AMF certificate. The firm must be registered. A lawyer, notary, CPA and so on is not a mortgage broker when doing brokerage in the exercise of their functions. No remuneration without a certificate. | LDPSF a. 12, 71, 11.2, 15 | https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/lc/D-9.2 |
| Mentions on the site: name, title, firm | In its advertising, the firm or independent representative uses its name (or the other names it uses in Québec), avoids a brand, slogan or wording that may cause confusion, and states the title under which it practises. At the first meeting the broker hands over a document (card) with name, business address, phone, firm name or "représentant autonome", authorized titles; at a distance, the broker communicates name, firm and titles. A site can carry these elements in the footer. | r 2 a. 1; r 10 a. 10, a. 12 | https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/rc/D-9.2,%20r.%202 |
| Registration or certificate number | No article found that requires it on a website. The AMF guide (secondary source) says one may give the certificate number or link to the Authority's registers. Exact wording of the guide to verify. | to verify | AMF guide above |
| Do not suggest AMF approval | AMF registration must not suggest that the broker's acts are approved or recognized by the AMF (no "AMF approved" logo, no "certifié AMF"). | r 2 a. 2 | https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/rc/D-9.2,%20r.%202 |
| Testimonials and client reviews | No article expressly forbids or allows them. The applicable frame: no false, misleading or error-inducing advertising by any means; do not dangle results you cannot deliver; the service must match the advertising. A fake or bought review is a misleading representation. The AMF guide may say more: to verify. | r 2 a. 3, 4, 5; r 10 a. 14, 16.13; LPC a. 219 | same sources |
| Fees and remuneration | Before the service, the broker discloses in writing the way they are paid (fees, conditions of payment, and the fact that they receive remuneration from the lender or a third party). They must explain it; fees must be fair and reasonable. No article requires showing a price on the site; if the site says "service gratuit", it must be accurate, since the broker is paid by the lender (disclosure required). | r 10 a. 9.3, 9.4, 16.11, 16.12; LDPSF a. 17 | https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/rc/D-9.2,%20r.%2010 |
| Titles and specialties | Only the titles provided by law may be used. Other elements may appear in representations if they cause no confusion, relate to the activity and are compatible: training, diplomas, titles obtained, years of experience by discipline, description of products and services. | r 2 a. 1; r 10 a. 11 | same sources |
| Comparisons and superlatives | Criticizing a competitor's products, services or working methods in advertising is forbidden. Advantages must not be stressed to the detriment of drawbacks. "Meilleur taux", "n° 1": stating one's financial performance or income is forbidden; for statistics, cite the source. The AMF guide (secondary) discourages mentions such as "vendeur n° 1 de l'année". | r 2 a. 8, 9, 6; r 10 a. 14 (1°), a. 13 | https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/rc/D-9.2,%20r.%202 |
| Result guarantees | Forbidden to dangle results the broker cannot deliver ("approbation garantie", "meilleur taux garanti"). | r 2 a. 3 (2°); r 10 a. 14 (2°) | same sources |
| Before and after, client cases | No specific rule. A client case is a representation subject to r 2 a. 5 and r 10 a. 14, and the broker's duty of confidentiality applies (a. 16.10). Anonymize and get the client's written consent. | r 10 a. 16.10; r 2 a. 5 | same sources |
| Displayed interest rates | A firm that advertises a financial product must get the authorization of whoever markets it (the lender). The AMF (notice 2020-04-09, secondary) says a displayed rate must be approved by the lender. The rate shown must be accurate, dated and accompanied by its conditions (the service must match the advertising). The LPC (a. 244, 247) governs credit advertising; whether it applies to a broker who does not grant the credit is to verify. | r 2 a. 10, a. 4, a. 5, a. 8; LPC a. 244, 247 (to verify) | same sources |
| Solicitation | The broker must refrain from any solicitation or representation likely to cause confusion. The firm may not adopt incentives that harm the client (contests steered toward specific products). The federal anti-spam law (CASL) applies to emails; not covered here. | r 10 a. 14; r 2 a. 11.1; LDPSF a. 202 (3°) | same sources |
| Social media | The rules cover "publicité, représentations et sollicitation" "par quelque moyen que ce soit": they apply to posts, videos and business pages. No article specific to social media found. | r 2 a. 5 | same sources |
| Business name and domain name | Use the registered name (or the other names used in Québec); no brand or slogan that may cause confusion. A franchisor must declare to the AMF the brands and names it lets its franchisees use. Nothing specific on domain names: apply a. 1 by analogy (avoid a domain suggesting a bank, the AMF or "tous les prêteurs"). To verify. | r 2 a. 1; r 2 a. 31 (franchise) | same sources |
| Advance payment | Sums received for others are deposited without delay in a separate account; receipt with the mentions of r 2 a. 28.2 for any sum collected. No rule on paying for an online consultation found; in Québec brokerage fees are usually paid by the lender. Verify before adding online payment. | r 10 a. 4 (2°), a. 9.2; r 2 a. 28.2 (text not re-read in detail) | https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/rc/D-9.2,%20r.%2010 |

## Contact form and Law 25 (mortgage broker)

For privacy mechanics use the `loi-25-website` skill. Points specific to a mortgage broker:

- No professional secrecy in the sense of the Code des professions (the broker is not a member of an order), but a regulatory duty of confidentiality: the broker keeps confidential the information obtained and uses it only for the purposes for which it was obtained (r 10 a. 16.10). The broker also records client information before proposing a loan (a. 9.7) and verifies identity (a. 9.8).
- Income, debts and credit history are probably "sensitive" (high expectation of privacy, P-39.1 a. 12), so express consent. Exact classification: to verify with the CAI.
- Contact form: name, phone, email, project type (purchase, renewal, refinancing) and city. Do not ask in the public form for income, debts, credit score, social insurance number, date of birth or ID documents; collect those later, during the needs analysis (a. 9.7) and identity check (a. 9.8), over a secure channel. Distinct consent, box not pre-ticked (P-39.1 a. 14).
- Hosting: no obligation to host in Québec. If information is communicated or stored outside Québec (host, CRM, email tool, analytics), a privacy impact assessment first (a. 17) and a written contract with the provider (a. 18.3). For a site offering online brokerage the AMF also asks to declare the hosting (secondary).
- Retention: regulation D-9.2, r 19 (books and records): duration not read, to verify.
